near a partially hyperbolic all-time lift. Its radius-dependent
constants do not give uniform diagonal-precision control.
Graph/cone and volume-distortion tools are existing methods.

and the high-side strict gap follow only AFTER (16)–(19). Partition
time dimension is not automatically the precision slope. This is a
comparison with the already read article, not a global novelty claim.
